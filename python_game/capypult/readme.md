##  Capypult

It is a single word, extremely easy to spell, instantly memorable, and immediately tells the player that they are playing a catapult/artillery game featuring capybaras.


## Gameplay & Archetype Mapping

Here is how the Capypult theme maps directly to the game's mechanics:

Capybaras Archetypes (Player Units)

* The City Capybaras (Standard): Balanced health and speed.
* The Park Capybaras (Heavy): Tanky, slow, fires heavier bread-crumb mortars.
* The Urban Capybaras (Tactician): Agile, lower health, specialized trick weapons.
